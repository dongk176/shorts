Each waits three seconds after lock acquisition before dispatch; the lock remains held through the response. A separate short-lived connection avoids application-pool deadlock when callers hold business transactions. Transaction locks are compatible with the existing transaction pooler. No session locks, business writes, provider credentials in lock keys, or in-memory-only fallback. Every request, including the first isolated request, incurs the three-second delay.

Admission has a 25-second deadline, DB lock waiting 20 seconds, provider HTTP/body timeout remains 15 seconds, and the lock transaction idle timeout is 25 seconds. Expired admissions cannot dispatch later. No automatic payment retry. Pre-dispatch failure is definite non-submission; failures after dispatch remain uncertain. Confirmed provider outcomes survive lock-transaction cleanup errors because that transaction contains no business writes. Connections close after each operation.
